CUSTOMER MATERIAL SPECIFICATION
================================

Document ref: SPEC-TC-2026-0042
Issued by:    [Customer A]
Issue date:   2026-01-15
Revision:     C
For part:     P/N TC-Bearing-Race-1107

This specification governs the material acceptance criteria for the part referenced above.
All material certificates from suppliers must be cross-checked against the values below
prior to part release. Any deviation must be flagged and discussed with the customer
before processing the material into production.

MATERIAL DESIGNATION
--------------------
Material:                 AISI 316L stainless steel
Standard reference:       ASTM A276-23a / ASTM A479-23a
Heat treatment condition: Solution annealed

MECHANICAL REQUIREMENTS (at room temperature)
---------------------------------------------
Tensile strength, min:    485 MPa
Yield strength (0.2%), min: 170 MPa
Elongation at break, min: 40%
Hardness, max:            HB 217

THERMAL TREATMENT REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
Solution annealing temperature range: 1040 to 1100 degrees C
Quench: rapid water quench mandatory
Cooling rate, min: 50 K/sec from solution temperature

CHEMICAL COMPOSITION (mass percent)
-----------------------------------
Carbon (C), max:      0.030 %
Manganese (Mn), max:  2.00 %
Phosphorus (P), max:  0.045 %
Sulfur (S), max:      0.030 %
Silicon (Si), max:    1.00 %
Chromium (Cr):        16.0 to 18.0 %
Nickel (Ni):          10.0 to 14.0 %
Molybdenum (Mo):      2.00 to 3.00 %
Nitrogen (N), max:    0.10 %

SURFACE FINISH REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------
Surface roughness Ra, max:  0.8 micrometres
Visual inspection:          No visible cracks, inclusions, or surface defects
                            permitted

DIMENSIONAL TRACEABILITY
------------------------
Heat number traceability:  Mandatory on all certificates
Mill source identification: Required (mill name + location)
Test report scope:         Per-heat results, signed by mill metallurgist

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------
Magnetic permeability, max: 1.05 (austenitic condition)
Intergranular corrosion test: ASTM A262 Practice E pass required
Charpy impact at -196 deg C, min: 60 J (3-specimen average)

END OF SPECIFICATION
